Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

Information We Collect

TreasureTrackr collects minimal information to provide you with the best experience:

  • Location data: When you grant permission, we use your device location to show nearby sales and calculate distances. This data is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers.
  • Sale listings: When you post a sale, we store the title, address, date, time, categories, items, and photos you provide.
  • Favorites and route data: Stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not have access to this data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To display nearby sales sorted by distance
  • To publish your sale listings so others can find them
  • To improve the app experience

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Sale listings are publicly visible to all app users.

Data Storage

Sale data is stored securely on Supabase servers. Local preferences (favorites, route stops, drafts) are stored only on your device.

Children's Privacy

TreasureTrackr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@treasuretrackr.com.
